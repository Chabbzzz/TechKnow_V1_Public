GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
before you explore.

Is my reservation confirmed right away?

A request becomes an accepted reservation when the shop confirms it. Follow the status in the app before making your visit.

Do I pay through TechKnow?

Payments are arranged directly with the shop. TechKnow helps you discover products, compare options, and send reservation requests.

How do I get my shop on TechKnow?

Create a store account and complete the permit submission process. If you already have an account, sign in to your store workspace.
